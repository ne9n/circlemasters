## 1. Maintain Website Domain

### AMA Recommendation
- Use easy & simple domain name (club name + .com, .org, .club)
- Choose club member to register, pay, and maintain domain
- Club should use shared email account for domain registration
- Register domain online (GoDaddy, WIX, ISP, etc.)
- Host website on appropriate server/ISP
- Create club officer email accounts from domain
- Domain should be movable between servers
