Frequently asked questions:
Q1 Who can start a meeting?   Anyone can start the meeting. 

What are the best practices?
  1. Check your microphones and speaker before the meeting stats.   10 minutes of "can you hear me?" takes everyone's time. 
  2. Please mute your system when not speaking.  Background noise is distracting and the system cant tell between noise and speech. 
  3. Everyone cannot talk at once.  This is especially true in a on line meeting.  Club officers run the meeting.  Wait to be recolonized to talk. 
  4. PC's work better.  While this application runs on phones and tablets,  your laptop to desktop makes it easier to use. 
  5. if you must use your phone, or tablet.  Download the jitsi app from the app store.  There are apps for android and Apple. 
    1. android app play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.jitsi.meet&hl=en_US&gl=US  
    2. Apple IOS app  apps.apple.com/us/app/jitsi-meet/id1165103905
  6. Use the Raise hand (lower left of screen) icon to get attention of the moderators.
  7. Use the text chat window to send a message to the group or an individual person.

How do i check my system? Checking your system. 

For windows 10 users 

to check camera got to start type camera and open the camera app

to check microphone 
  1. Make sure your microphone is connected to your PC.
  2. Select Start   > Settings  > System > Sound.
  3. In Sound settings, go to Input > Choose your input device, and then select the microphone or recording device you want to use.  
To test a microphone that has already been installed:
  1. Make sure your microphone is connected to your PC.
  2. Select Start   > Settings  > System > Sound.
  3. In Sound settings, go to Input > Test your microphone and look for the blue bar that rises and falls as you speak into your microphone. If the bar is moving, your microphone is working properly.
