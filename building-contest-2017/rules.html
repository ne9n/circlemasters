Circle Masters 2017 building contest
  • 3 classes, sport competition and  1/2a challenge$5 one class $10 multiple entries multiple, classes
  • Enter by Dec 3 2017
Proceeds split between picnic for contestants and field box fund
Sport and Competition Models general  
  • Judging by contestants (form provided)
  • Any new construction must start after September 1 2017.  No rebuilds, ARF’s or old projects.
  • Control line models only. 
  • Kits, plans or original designs are allowed.
  • Model must be constructed, finished and flown by June 15. Proxy flying encouraged. Video allowed 
  • Must provide photos and bring projects to the winter meetings.  Pictures will be posted.
  • Enter as many times as you wish one winner  
Cox engine challenge
  • Rules from above plus
  • Build a model based on a cox product engine and fly it
  • Flight is 3 laps minimum.
