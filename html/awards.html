Awards

AMA Gold Leader Club

The Milwaukee Circle Masters Flying Club is an Academy of Model Aeronautics (AMA) Chartered Club #662 and has been recognized as an AMA Gold Leader Club for over 25 consecutive years.

Spotlight: Model Aviation (November 2019)

Source: Model Aviation Digital — November 2019 Issue

Leader Clubs: 25 Consecutive Years

"FIVE AMA CLUBS have a reason to celebrate this year. They have been Leader Clubs for 25 consecutive years! In order to be named an AMA Leader Club, organizations must be chartered by AMA for a minimum of 5 years, have their club’s membership open to all AMA members, and must develop and post safety and operation rules at their flying sites and provide this information to AMA Headquarters. The club’s field also needs separate areas for fliers and spectators and these areas have to be clearly marked. The AMA Safety Code must be posted and visible at the field."

"Congratulations to the following clubs for meeting these qualifications for 25 years: the Rochester Aero-Modeling Society, New York; the Tuscarora R/C Flying Club, Pennsylvania; Skymasters R/C of Michigan; the Circle Masters Flying Club, Wisconsin; and the Jeffco Aeromod’lers, Colorado."

More info: www.modelaircraft.org/clubs/recognition-rewards/ama-leader-clubs

What is an AMA Leader Club?

In an effort to recognize the leadership of the finest and most dedicated AMA chartered clubs, the Academy offers the Leader Club Awards. These awards are earned by chartered clubs that demonstrate the highest standards of community service, safety, open membership, and modeling activity. Clubs can achieve Bronze, Silver, and Gold status.

Elite Distinction

While there are over 100 Gold Leader Clubs across the country, only a select few maintain this status continuously for over two decades. The Milwaukee Circle Masters is one of only 14 clubs nationwide — and the only dedicated Control Line club — to achieve Gold Leader status for over 25 consecutive years!
