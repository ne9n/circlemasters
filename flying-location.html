About Our Flying Field

Welcome to the Circle Masters Flying Club's official flying location! This page provides you with all the essential details about our flying site, situated at the scenic Sussex Village Park in Sussex, Wisconsin. Below you'll find the current weather conditions to help you plan your flights, as well as an interactive map for easy navigation to the park. Please review our site guidelines below to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone.

Site Guidelines & Details:
• Space is shared and may not be available at all times.
• Located at the corner of Park and Weaver Drive.
• Amenities include a square paved center, bleachers, picnic tables, water, and portable toilets.
• Please park next to the Lions building.
• Be alert for walkers, dogs, and Frisbee golfers.
• Mufflers must be used.
• Site is limited to control line flight ONLY.
