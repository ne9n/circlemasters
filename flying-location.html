The Club flying location is Sussex Village Park in Sussex Wisconsin
Space is shared space and may not be available at all times.
Location at the corner of Park and Weaver Drive
Site has Square paved center, bleachers, picnic tables water and porti johns
Park next to the Lions building
Be alert for walkers, dogs and Frisbee golfers  
Mufflers must be used.
Site is limited to control line flight ONLY
